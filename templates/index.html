Privacy, Data Handling & AI Usage Policy

Zero Local Retention

LectureLens does not store transcripts or audio recordings on the server. Audio files uploaded for transcription are removed immediately after processing.

Google Gemini Processing

Transcripts and audio are transmitted securely to Google's Gemini API for inference. Processing is subject to Google Cloud & AI Studio data terms.

API Quotas & Billing

Gemini API usage, speed, and quotas depend on your own Google AI Studio account. Free tier quotas apply according to Google's current terms.

Academic Integrity Disclaimer

AI-synthesized notes are study aids designed to assist revision. Students must always cross-reference primary lecture materials, textbooks, and course syllabi.
